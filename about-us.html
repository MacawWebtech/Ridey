01 / OUR STORY2026

A shorter distance
deserved a better answer.

Traffic, parking and rigid rental plans made everyday movement harder than it needed to be. Ridey was shaped around the gap between walking and taking a car: quick scooters, clear pricing and pickup points that fit real routines.
